# Contadino positioning brief

Approved through the grilling interview, November 1, 2026.

Promise: make room for focused work and see where your time went.
Voice: calm, thoughtful, warm, direct; no guilt or optimization coaching.
Audience: work, study, and personal projects, without a narrow professional persona.
Story: start a session, stay with the task, look back; notes and tags provide context.
App Store: about 150–200 words, evergreen and platform-specific; updates stay in What’s New.
Website: the same story with space and authentic images. Preserve yellow identity. Lead with iPhone and Mac, show history, then themes and other devices. No App Store headlines baked into imagery.
Purchase explanation: retain the plain explanation near the end of the website. Following review, omit the Contadino+ purchase paragraph from all App Store descriptions.

## Review boundary

The approved descriptions target the en-US 1.4.0 draft localizations in Helm. Website publication is approved. App Store submission, existing privacy policy, and subscription availability decisions remain separate.

## Screenshot provenance

See contadino-image-provenance.json. Captures are real app UI with fictional sandbox data; website assets are proportional reductions of raw captures. No new UI was generated or composited into the screenshots.
