# Contadino screenshot artwork

These are the reviewed direction A assets: authentic app captures in sourced
frames-cli 1.5.1 artwork, with Encode Sans and ivory/yellow page composition.
The desktop hero uses physically scaled Mac and iPhone artwork without overlap;
mobile uses one large phone. Themes stack on mobile for legibility.

Mac History is a native window capture with the full calendar and real system
buttons, composed over installed Sonoma wallpaper. Only contaminated corner alpha
is restored; app RGB pixels are unchanged. Its week shows daily counts
2, 5, 3, 6, 3, 5, 4; selected day remains four sessions / 1h 43m. Vision retains the
complete native Simulator scene without a bezel. Other app pages keep their styles.

Source: https://github.com/viticci/frames-cli (tool and asset pack); Apple design
resources and marketing artwork guidelines apply. Device artwork is proportional,
uncropped and unobstructed. The old synthetic CSS bezels have been removed,
including the homepage wrapper around the now preframed phone.

Asset hashes and processing are recorded in `contadino-screenshot-assets.json`.
Generated review HTML and local filesystem paths are not shipped.
