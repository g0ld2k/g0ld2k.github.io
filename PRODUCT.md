# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is Apple-device users discovering and evaluating the developer's apps. Existing app users also need access to support and privacy information. The secondary audience is people interested in the developer and their work.

## Product Purpose

g0ld2k.com helps visitors understand what each app does, decide whether it suits their needs, and reach its installation destination. It also showcases the independent developer through the apps they make.

Success primarily means useful app discovery and a clear path to installation; recognition of the developer is secondary. No quantitative success targets have been established.

## Positioning

A solo developer's collection of Apple-platform apps addressing everyday needs: focus, distance goals, and coordination across time zones. The existing product story is grounded in tools the developer makes for problems they encounter personally.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the app collection, explore an app's capabilities and supported devices, and follow its App Store or TestFlight link. Returning users can find app-specific contact details, privacy policies, and available support material.

The site is a Jekyll website deployed through GitHub Pages. Its web platform is distinct from the native Apple platforms supported by the apps it showcases.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current repository describes three apps:

- **Zoner:** time zone coordination with locations, people, groups, working-hours awareness, iCloud sync, backup import/export, widgets, and a macOS menu bar helper. Its page describes Mac and iPhone support and links to TestFlight.
- **Contadino:** Pomodoro-style focus tracking, session and interruption history, adjustable focus/break durations, and statistics across macOS, iOS, iPadOS, visionOS, and watchOS. Its page links to the App Store.
- **Distance Track:** walking, running, and cycling distance goals using Apple Health workouts, with progress visualization on iPhone and iPad. Its page links to the App Store.

These are repository-backed descriptions, not independent verification of the native apps or live store availability. Consult each app's current page when changing feature, platform, or distribution claims.

Preserve app-specific support destinations and access to privacy policies and terms. App data storage and privacy claims must remain specific to the relevant app and supported by its policy.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the g0ld2k.com identity, existing app names, and solo-developer identity. The user prefers bold, fun colors and designs, but is not attached to the previous terminal/neon aesthetic. The approved scope is a full-site redesign. Existing app imagery is evidence of the products being showcased.

## Evidence on Hand

- `index.md`: the collection's current introduction and app summaries.
- `Zoner/index.md`, `Contadino/index.md`, and `DistanceTrack/index.md`: app capabilities, platform descriptions, installation links, and contact destinations.
- `assets/images/`: app screenshots, device compositions, and identity assets.
- App-specific privacy pages, `Contadino/tou.md`, and `Zoner/support.md`: existing policy and support material.

Known content issue: `DistanceTrack/privacy.md` currently names Contadino and uses Contadino's contact address. Its app-specific accuracy remains unresolved; do not treat that text as verified Distance Track policy content.

No testimonials, customer counts, performance benchmarks, or awards were established during initialization.

## Product Principles

1. Help visitors find an app that addresses their needs; let the actual work establish the developer's credibility.
2. Explain capabilities and supported platforms plainly so users can make informed installation decisions.
3. Keep release status and installation destinations accurate as the apps evolve.
4. Make support and privacy information easy to find and faithful to each app.

## Open Decisions

No product-specific accessibility standard or additional audience requirements were established during initialization.
